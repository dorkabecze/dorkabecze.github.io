DORKA BECZE : Moholy-Nagy Editorial
[ Next ] [ Close ]

A redesign of Krisztina Passuth’s book Moholy-Nagy, exploring transparency and glass architecture as the central visual concepts. Inspired by Moholy-Nagy’s experiments with light, space, and perception, the project translates architectural qualities into a graphic language through overlapping elements, translucent layers, and optical depth.

Consultants: Eszter Misztarka, Simon Peter Bence
