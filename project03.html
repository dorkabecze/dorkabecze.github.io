DORKA BECZE : Uborka Type Specimen
[ Next ] [ Close ]

An interactive type specimen website showcasing Uborka, a typeface designed by Ádám Katyi. The concept explores the tension between the expressive freedom of pointed-pen calligraphy and the rigid structure of monospaced typography. Through playful animations, interactive elements, and ink-inspired visuals, the website transforms traditional type presentation into an engaging digital experience, inviting users to discover the typeface’s unique characteristics through exploration.
