DORKA BECZE : Typoflora
[ Next ] [ Close ]

Typoflora explores the relationship between nature and technology through experimental typography. Using a scanner, I transformed real plants into letterforms, capturing how light and pressure flatten, distort, and reshape their organic structures. The result is a visual archive of unique botanical impressions.
