DORKA BECZE : City of God
[ Next ] [ Close ]

An experimental title sequence inspired by Fernando Meirelles’ City of God, exploring photography as a tool for survival, observation, and documenting reality. Through fragmented compositions, high-contrast black-and-white imagery, and raw collage techniques, the project translates the film’s chaotic urban atmosphere into a rhythmic visual narrative. Harsh cuts and analog-inspired textures reflect the tension between violence, everyday life, and human connection, capturing the film’s intense energy and non-linear storytelling.

Consultants: Adrienn Császár, Köllőd Márton
